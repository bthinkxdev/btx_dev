Get the app on your phone

It opens full screen like any other app and tells you the moment something needs you.

  1. 1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. 2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. 3. Open the app from your home screen, then turn on notifications.
