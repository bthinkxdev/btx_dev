================================================================================
BTHINKX BUSINESS OS — USER MANUAL
ROLE: Team Member (Developer / Designer / Tester)
================================================================================
Your access: OWN scope — the projects you're on, the tasks assigned to you,
the tickets assigned to you, and your own leave/attendance/expenses.
Your home screen after login: My Day.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIGNING IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enter your email/username + password, complete 2FA if prompted (6-digit
code, 5-minute window). Forgot your password? Ask your manager or Owner —
you cannot self-reset.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MY DAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your personal daily list — what's due today across your tasks. Start here
every day before opening anything else.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PROJECTS (view only, the ones you're on) — Delivery -> Projects
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
See the projects you're a member of: client, timeline, milestones, and
where your assigned tasks sit. You cannot edit project details — that's
your Project Manager's job (manual 08).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TASKS — Delivery -> Tasks
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Status lifecycle
  To do -> In progress -> (Blocked) -> In review -> Done (or Cancelled)

4.2 Working your tasks
  1) Open a task assigned to you from Tasks or from My Day.
  2) Move it to "In progress" when you start.
  3) If something stops you, set it to "Blocked" and log a Blocker
     (Delivery -> Blockers -> Add, or from the task) with a clear
     description — don't just leave it stalled silently.
  4) Move to "In review" when done and ready for your manager/lead to
     check; "Done" once confirmed, or back to "In progress" if it's sent
     back.
  5) You can also create your own tasks (e.g. a sub-task you've identified)
     — Tasks -> Add task, project + title + due date.
Must do: keep status current in real time, not at end of week — your
  Project Manager's utilisation and delivery reporting reads directly off
  this, and a stale "To do" that's actually done hides real progress.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TIMESHEETS — Delivery -> Timesheet
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status lifecycle: Draft -> Submitted -> Approved (or Rejected)
  1) Log hours against the task/project you worked on, for the day/week.
  2) Save as Draft while you're still filling it in; "Submit" once the
     period is complete and accurate — this sends it to your manager for
     approval.
  3) If rejected, check the reason, correct it, and resubmit.
Must do: submit your timesheet on the cadence your team uses (usually
  weekly) — approvals and billing both wait on it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. TICKETS (assigned to you) — Delivery -> Tickets
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status lifecycle: Open -> In progress -> Waiting for client -> Resolved ->
  Closed
  Work tickets assigned to you the same way as tasks — move status as you
  progress, and watch for the "Breached" badge (SLA due has passed) which
  means it needs attention right now, ahead of everything else non-urgent.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. YOUR OWN LEAVE, ATTENDANCE AND EXPENSES (same for every role)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Leave — People -> Leave
  1) "Apply for leave" — pick leave type, start/end dates, reason.
  2) Check your leave balance shown on the form before applying.
  3) Submit — status Pending until your manager Approves/Rejects it; you
     can "Cancel" your own request while still Pending.

7.2 Attendance — People -> Attendance
  1) Check in when you start work, check out when you finish (button on
     the Attendance screen, or however your company captures it e.g.
     biometric import).
  2) If a check-in/out was missed or wrong, raise a "Correction" request
     with the right time and a reason — it goes to HR/your manager to
     approve.

7.3 Expenses — People -> Expenses -> Add expense
  1) Choose kind: Expense / Vendor bill / Reimbursement.
  2) Pick category, enter amount, attach the receipt (required).
  3) Submit. Status moves Draft -> Submitted -> Approved -> Paid (or
     Rejected / Correction requested — fix what's flagged and resubmit).
Must do: always attach a receipt before submitting; claims with no proof
  are routinely sent back.

7.4 Your payslips — People -> Payroll -> My payslips
  View and download your own payslips once a payroll run is released.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The bell icon shows task assignments, approval decisions on your own
requests, and anything due. Click to jump straight to it, "Snooze" if
you'll deal with it later, "Mark all read" once triaged.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. YOUR DAILY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] My Day — what's due today.
  [ ] Update status on any task/ticket you worked on (don't batch this up).
  [ ] Log a Blocker the moment something stops you, don't wait.
  [ ] Check in/out for attendance.
  [ ] Notifications bell — triage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I can't see a project/task" -> you only see ones you're a member of or
  assigned to; ask your Project Manager to add you.
"I can't edit the project details" -> correct, by design — that's your
  Project Manager's permission; ask them for the change.
"My leave/expense request is stuck" -> it's Pending until your manager
  decides; check with them directly if it's been a while.
"My timesheet was rejected" -> open it, read the rejection reason, correct
  the hours/task/project, and resubmit.
