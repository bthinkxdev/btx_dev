================================================================================
BTHINKX BUSINESS OS — USER MANUAL
ROLE: Telecaller / Lead Coordinator
================================================================================
Your access: COMPANY scope on Leads (view/create/edit/assign/import), view
of Pipeline and the Service catalogue, and your OWN Leave/Attendance. You do
not have access to quotations, payments, invoices, clients, or reports —
your job is getting every enquiry in, qualified, and into the right rep's
hands fast.
Your home screen after login: Leads -> All leads.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIGNING IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enter your email/username + password, complete 2FA if prompted. Forgot your
password? Ask your Sales Manager or Owner — you cannot self-reset.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. YOUR CORE JOB, IN ORDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1) Triage the Inbound inbox (new enquiries from forms/ads/missed calls).
  2) Create/clean up leads from phone enquiries that didn't come in through
     a form.
  3) Qualify: confirm the contact is real, capture what they actually want.
  4) Assign each lead to the right sales rep without delay.
  5) Catch and merge duplicates before they confuse two reps.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. INBOUND INBOX — Leads -> Inbound inbox
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every raw hit from a website form, Meta/Google lead ad, or missed call
lands here first, with a status: Received -> (processing) -> Converted /
Discarded.
  1) Open an item — review the raw details captured (name, phone, message,
     source).
  2) "Convert to lead" if it's real; this creates a Lead you can then edit,
     or "Discard" (with a reason) if it's spam/test/wrong number.
  3) If a delivery attempt failed (e.g. webhook retry), the row shows a
     retry count and next-retry time — leave it, the system retries
     automatically; only escalate to Owner if it keeps failing.
Must do: clear the inbox daily — the longer a real enquiry sits unconverted,
the worse your first-response-time looks for the whole company, and it is
measured.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CREATING A LEAD MANUALLY — Leads -> All leads -> Add lead
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) Fill name, phone, company (if any), service interested, and source/
   channel: Website form, Meta lead form, Google Ads, Phone call, Missed
   call, Email, WhatsApp, Manual entry, Referral, Existing client, Other.
2) The system checks for likely duplicates by phone/email as you type —
   resolve the match (it's probably the same person calling again) before
   creating a new record.
3) Save, then assign an owner (step 6) — don't leave it unassigned.
Must do: always set the real source. "Manual entry" should only be used
when you genuinely cannot tell where the lead came from; everything else
has a specific, correct option.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. QUALIFYING A LEAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the lead's detail page, use Quick log (Log call / Log meeting / Add
note / Log email or WhatsApp) to record what you learned — budget signal,
timeline, decision-maker, exact service needed. Set Priority (Hot / Warm /
Cold) based on how ready-to-buy they sound; this is what tells the
receiving rep what to do first. Move the stage to "Contacted" once you've
actually spoken to them, or "Qualified" once you're confident it's a real
opportunity worth a rep's time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. ASSIGNING LEADS — Leads -> All leads
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Single lead: open it -> "⋮" menu -> Assign, or "Change stage"/owner field
  on the detail page.
- Many at once: Table view -> tick the checkboxes -> the bulk bar appears
  at the top -> "Assign selected" -> choose owner -> Apply.
- The "Unassigned" quick filter on the list finds every lead still waiting
  for a rep — run this filter first thing every session.
- Every open lead must have a scheduled next follow-up; set one via
  "Schedule follow-up" if the receiving rep hasn't yet, so nothing goes
  cold waiting for them to pick it up.
Must do: never leave a qualified lead unassigned overnight.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DUPLICATES — Leads -> Duplicates
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) Click "Scan" to find likely duplicate leads (matching phone/email).
2) Open a pair -> "Compare" — pick which fields to keep from each side.
3) Merge. Statuses on the list: Open (needs review), Merged, Not a
   duplicate, Review later.
Must do: run a scan at least weekly, and always just after a bulk import.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. IMPORTING LEADS IN BULK — Leads -> Import leads
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) Download the CSV template (Import leads -> Download template).
2) Fill it in exactly to the template's columns; upload the file.
3) Map your columns to system fields on the Map screen.
4) Validate — the system flags rows with missing/bad data; fix your source
   file and re-upload the problem rows, or proceed and skip them.
5) Run the import, then check the Result screen (how many created/skipped/
   duplicated). If something went wrong, "Undo" reverts the whole batch —
   do this quickly, before reps start working the new leads.
Must do: always run the Duplicates scan right after a CSV import.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. SOURCES AND CAMPAIGNS — Leads -> Sources and campaigns
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Keep the list of sources/campaigns you tag leads against accurate and
agreed with your Sales Manager — this feeds every source-performance
report in the company. Don't invent new source names on the fly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. STALE AND UNASSIGNED LEADS — Leads -> Stale and unassigned
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Leads that have had no activity for a while, or no owner. This is your
daily cleanup list, alongside the "Unassigned" quick filter.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. TRACKING YOUR WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Quick filters on Leads -> All leads: Unassigned, Due today, Overdue, No
activity 3 days, New in last 24h, High priority, Open, Not yet contacted,
First-response SLA breached. There is no personal dashboard for this role
— your manager's Sales dashboard reports on response time and lead
hand-off speed, which is driven entirely by how fast you work the inbox
and assign.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. YOUR OWN LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
People -> Leave: apply for leave; it goes to your manager to approve.
People -> Attendance: check in/out each day; request a correction if a
check-in was missed or wrong.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. YOUR DAILY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] Inbound inbox — convert or discard everything new.
  [ ] Leads -> Unassigned quick filter — assign everything.
  [ ] Leads -> First-response SLA breached — chase/escalate immediately.
  [ ] Duplicates — scan if you did a bulk import or a busy ad day.
  [ ] Stale and unassigned — redistribute anything slipping.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I can't see quotations/clients/payments" -> correct, by design — that is
  outside your role; ask a Sales Executive/Manager for that information.
"The same person keeps submitting the form" -> check Duplicates; merge
  them into one lead instead of working two records.
"An inbound item keeps failing to convert" -> leave it, the system retries
  automatically; escalate to Owner only if it stays failed after several
  retries.
"I assigned a lead to the wrong person" -> reassign it correctly, then use
  "Undo" on that assignment entry in the lead's Timeline if it was done
  moments ago, otherwise just assign it again to the right person.
