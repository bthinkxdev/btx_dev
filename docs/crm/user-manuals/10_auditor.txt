================================================================================
BTHINKX BUSINESS OS — USER MANUAL
ROLE: Auditor
================================================================================
Your access: READ-ONLY, COMPANY-wide, across every module except Payroll,
Employees and Settings (you cannot see salary/bank/payroll detail or change
system configuration). You additionally have export rights on Reports and
view access to the Audit log. You create and change nothing in this system
— your job is to look, verify, and export evidence.
Your home screen after login: Dashboards -> Owner.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIGNING IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enter your email/username + password, complete 2FA if prompted (6-digit
code, 5-minute window). Forgot your password? Ask the Owner — you cannot
self-reset.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT YOU CAN DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Open and read any record in Leads, Pipeline, Quotations, Payment links,
    Payments, Invoices, Receipts, Refunds, Clients, Services, Contracts,
    Projects, Tasks, Tickets, Expenses, Vendors, Accounting, Banking,
    Leave, Commissions — company-wide, not just one team's.
  - Read every record's Timeline/history tab — this is your primary
    evidence trail: who did what, when, and (for destructive/approval
    actions) why.
  - View and export every report in the Report catalogue.
  - View the Audit log (Settings -> Audit) — the system's own immutable
    log of every action taken by every user.
  - Export CSV from any list or report screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT YOU CANNOT DO (and why)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - No create/edit/delete anywhere — you are a read/export-only role by
    design, so your presence never changes what you're reviewing.
  - No access to Payroll or Employee records (salary, bank details, tax
    IDs are excluded) — these stay restricted to HR Manager and Owner.
  - No access to Settings (users, roles, company/tax/gateway config) —
    system configuration is an Owner-only function.
  - If you hit a 403 "You do not have access to..." on something outside
    this list, that is expected, not a bug — it is simply outside an
    auditor's scope by design.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TO ACTUALLY AUDIT SOMETHING, STEP BY STEP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Tracing one transaction end to end (e.g. a payment)
  1) Find it: use the top-bar search (Ctrl/Cmd+K), or the module's list
     with filters, or start from the Client it belongs to (Client detail
     shows every related lead/quotation/invoice/payment/project in one
     place).
  2) Open the record, read its Timeline — every status change, who made
     it, when, and the reason given for any destructive/approval action.
  3) Follow the links on the record to its related documents (e.g. a
     Payment links back to its Invoice/Quotation and to any Gateway
     events/Settlement it was matched against during reconciliation).
  4) Cross-check against Settings -> Audit log for the same user/date/
     module if you need the raw, unfiltered system event, not just the
     record's own view of its history.

4.2 Verifying a control is actually working (e.g. "payments require
  verification before counting as revenue")
  1) Pick a sample of Payments -> filter to Verified.
  2) Open each — confirm a Pending verification -> Verified transition
     exists in its Timeline, with a named verifier different from whoever
     recorded it.
  3) Spot-check that nothing reached Verified without going through
     Pending first (would indicate a control bypass — escalate to Owner
     immediately if found).

4.3 Verifying approval limits are respected (e.g. discounts/refunds)
  1) Open Settings -> Audit (or the relevant report) filtered to "approve"
     actions for the period.
  2) For each, confirm the approver is not the same person who requested
     it, and that items above the configured policy limit actually went
     through an approval step rather than straight to Approved/Issued.

4.4 Checking for forbidden-access attempts
  Settings -> Security -> Security events shows every 403 (forbidden)
  event — someone trying an action their role doesn't permit. A pattern of
  these from one account is worth flagging to Owner even though it did not
  succeed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. REPORTS — Reports (company scope, export enabled)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The full Report catalogue is your fastest route to evidence: receivables
ageing, GST/TDS summaries, sales funnel/win-loss, expense analysis, vendor
spend, delivery utilisation, headcount (where visible). Open a report, set
the exact date range the audit period requires, and Export CSV for your
workpapers. "Schedule" a report if you need the same export delivered
automatically on a recurring basis during a long engagement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE AUDIT LOG, IN DETAIL — Settings -> Audit
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every entry shows: who, what module/action, on which record, when, and
from where (IP/session where captured). Filter by user, module or date
range. Treat this as the system of record over any report or dashboard
figure if the two ever disagree — the audit log is the raw event stream
everything else is computed from.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. YOUR WORKING CHECKLIST FOR AN ENGAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] Define the period and modules in scope.
  [ ] Pull the relevant reports for the period, export as your baseline.
  [ ] Sample transactions across each in-scope module; trace each one's
      Timeline end to end (section 4.1).
  [ ] Verify key controls (payment verification, approval-before-limit,
      numbering-series integrity, period close discipline) against the
      Audit log (section 4.2-4.3).
  [ ] Review Security events for the period for anomalies.
  [ ] Document findings with record IDs/numbers and audit-log timestamps
      so Owner can verify or respond to each one directly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I need to see something in Payroll/Employees" -> that's outside your
  role by design; request it formally through the Owner, who can pull it
  or temporarily extend access with a clear audit trail of why.
"A number in a report doesn't match what I see in the list" -> check the
  date range and any filters on each side match exactly; also check for
  records outside your scope that might differ (should not happen at
  company scope, but confirm) before treating it as a real discrepancy.
"I can't find a record" -> try the global search (Ctrl/Cmd+K) with any
  identifier you have (name, phone, invoice number, amount won't search
  directly — use the relevant list's filters for amount-based lookups).
