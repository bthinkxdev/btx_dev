================================================================================
BTHINKX BUSINESS OS — USER MANUAL
ROLE: Accountant
================================================================================
Your access: COMPANY scope and full control of Accounting (incl. configure/
override) and Banking (incl. approve); COMPANY on Invoices (incl. approve/
cancel/export/override), Receipts, Payments (incl. approve), Payment links
(approve), Refunds (approve/execute), Expenses (approve/export), Audit log
(view); COMPANY view on Quotations (+override), Clients, Services
(+configure), Contracts, Vendors, Reports (+export); OWN Leave/Attendance.
You own the books — journals, ledgers, reconciliation, period close,
statutory reports.
Your home screen after login: Accounting -> Dashboard.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIGNING IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enter your email/username + password, complete 2FA if prompted. Forgot your
password? Ask your Owner — you cannot self-reset.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ACCOUNTING DASHBOARD — Accounting -> Dashboard
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Shows posting health (how many transactions are queued/failed to post to
the ledger), period status, and a snapshot of the trial balance. "Retry
postings" re-attempts any failed automatic postings (e.g. an invoice that
should have created a journal entry but didn't) — run this first if a
number looks off anywhere downstream.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CHART OF ACCOUNTS — Accounting -> Accounts
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Asset / Liability / Equity / Income / Expense accounts. "Add account" to
create a new one (code, name, type, parent if sub-account); "Edit" to
rename/reclassify; "Deactivate" to retire an account you no longer post to
(history stays, you just can't post new entries to it).
Must do: agree any chart-of-accounts change with your Owner before making
it — restating the structure after real postings exist complicates every
historical report.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. JOURNAL ENTRIES — Accounting -> Journals
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Status lifecycle
  Draft -> Pending approval -> Posted (or Reversed)
  Most journal entries post automatically from source transactions
  (invoices, payments, expenses, payroll) — you mainly create manual
  entries for adjustments, accruals, depreciation, and corrections.

4.2 Creating a manual journal
  1) Journals -> New journal — enter narration, date, and debit/credit
     lines against accounts; debits must equal credits before you can
     save.
  2) Save as Draft, review.
  3) "Post" to commit it to the ledger (above your policy limit it instead
     goes to Pending approval first).
  4) To correct a posted entry, never edit it — open it and "Reverse": this
     creates an exact opposite entry dated today, tagged as a reversal of
     the original, then post a corrected entry. "Discard" only applies to
     an entry still in Draft/Pending approval that was never posted.
Must do: every manual journal needs a clear narration — "adjustment" is not
  enough; write what it's for and reference the source document/ticket.

4.3 General ledger — Accounting -> Ledger
  Every posted line, filterable by account and date range — your main tool
  for investigating "why does this account show this balance".

4.4 Trial balance / P&L / Balance sheet / Cash flow / Tax reports —
  Accounting -> Trial balance / Profit and loss / Balance sheet / Cash flow
  / Tax
  Standard financial statements, each with a date-range/period selector and
  CSV export. Tax reports covers your GST and TDS summaries for filing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PERIOD CLOSE — Accounting -> Periods
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status lifecycle: Open -> Soft-closed -> Closed
  - "Soft close" a period once you've reconciled everything in it but want
    a short buffer before fully locking it — postings are flagged/warned
    but not yet fully blocked.
  - "Close" locks the period completely — no new postings against it;
    corrections must go through the current open period instead.
  - "Reopen" only in an emergency, with a reason — this is logged and
    should be rare and agreed with your Owner.
Must do: close the prior month only after bank reconciliation, invoice/
  expense approvals, and payroll for that month are all finalised.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. BANKING AND RECONCILIATION — Banking -> Accounts / Reconciliation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Bank accounts — add/maintain the company's bank accounts.
6.2 Import a statement — Banking -> Reconciliation -> Import: upload the
  bank's exported statement file for the period.
6.3 Matching — each line starts Unmatched; "Auto match" suggests matches
  against payments/expenses by amount+date+reference (Suggested); for each
  suggestion, "Confirm" if correct. For anything left:
  - "Match" it manually to an existing payment/expense.
  - "Create" a new expense/journal line directly from the bank line if
    nothing in the system corresponds to it yet (e.g. a bank charge).
  - "Ignore" a line that's genuinely not yours to reconcile (rare).
  - "Unmatch" to undo an incorrect match before finalising.
6.4 Finalise — Banking -> Reconciliation -> Finalise once every line for
  the period is resolved. This locks the reconciliation; "Undo" is
  available only shortly after, from the reconciliation's own page — after
  that, correct via a new entry in the current period instead.
6.5 Reconciliation report — open any past reconciliation to see its
  finalised detail, e.g. for an audit request.
Must do: reconcile every bank account every period before soft-closing it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. INVOICES, PAYMENTS, PAYMENT LINKS, REFUNDS (full company control)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You hold the same day-to-day actions described in the Finance Executive
manual (05, sections 3-5) at company scope, plus:
  - "Override" on an invoice/quotation — forces a status/total outside the
    normal workflow for a genuine correction case. Always logged; use only
    when a credit note or reversal genuinely cannot express the fix.
  - Final approval authority on payment links, refunds and invoice credit
    notes/cancellations above Finance Executive's own limit.
Must do: treat Override as a last resort, not a shortcut — prefer a Credit
  note, a Reversal, or a Refund, which all leave a cleaner trail.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. EXPENSES AND VENDORS (approve + export)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same screens as Finance Executive manual (05, section 7); you additionally
have export rights company-wide for audit/filing purposes and can approve
anything a Finance Executive has escalated or is unavailable to clear.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. SERVICE CATALOGUE CONFIGURATION — Sales -> Services
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can configure tax rules per service (Services -> a service -> Tax
rules): taxability, GST rate, price mode (inclusive/exclusive), effective
dates. "End" a tax rule when a rate changes rather than editing the old
one, so historical documents keep the rate that applied when they were
issued.
Must do: any GST rate change goes live only from a clear effective date —
  never retroactively alter a rule that already has documents against it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. AUDIT LOG — Settings -> Audit (view)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full, immutable history of who did what, when. Use this to investigate any
discrepancy before assuming data is wrong — it usually tells you exactly
which action changed a number and who did it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. REPORTS (company scope, export enabled)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every financial report in the catalogue, exportable and schedulable. Use
Schedule to have your month-end pack (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, tax
summary) land automatically for the Owner each month-end.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. YOUR OWN LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
People -> Leave / Attendance — same self-service screens every employee
uses; see manual 09, section 7.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. APPROVALS — YOUR INBOX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Journal approvals, invoice/credit-note approvals above policy limit,
payment link/refund approvals, reconciliation sign-offs where configured.
Clear "Mine" daily.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. YOUR MONTH-END CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] All bank accounts reconciled for the period.
  [ ] All Expense approvals and Payment verifications for the period clear.
  [ ] Accounting -> Retry postings run, zero failed postings remain.
  [ ] Trial balance reviewed for any account with an unexpected balance.
  [ ] P&L, Balance sheet, Cash flow reviewed and exported.
  [ ] Tax reports reconciled against what you intend to file.
  [ ] Period soft-closed, then fully closed once confirmed clean.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"An invoice didn't create a journal entry" -> Accounting -> Dashboard ->
  "Retry postings"; if it still fails, check the invoice's own status/
  totals for something invalid before escalating.
"Trial balance doesn't balance" -> check for a Draft/Pending-approval
  journal you expected posted, and for any entry posted into the wrong
  period (check its date against period boundaries).
"Can't post into a period" -> it's Closed; use the current open period for
  the correction, or Reopen only with Owner agreement.
"Reconciliation line won't match anything" -> "Create" a new entry from the
  bank line directly (e.g. an unrecorded bank charge) rather than leaving
  it Unmatched indefinitely.
