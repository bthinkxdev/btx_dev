How to use this guide
================================================================================

This folder contains one complete user manual per role (user type) in the
system. Every manual is self-contained — you only need to read the one that
matches your role(s).

WHICH ONE IS MINE?
  Your role is shown next to your name in the top-right corner of the app,
  and under Settings -> Users -> (your name). If you hold more than one role
  (e.g. Sales Manager + Project Manager), read both manuals — your screen
  will show the combined menu of both.

WHAT "SCOPE" MEANS (you will see this word in every manual)
  Every permission in this system is granted at one of four scopes:
    OWN        - only records where you are the owner / assignee
    TEAM       - your own records plus your team's
    DEPARTMENT - your own, your team's, and your department's
    COMPANY    - every record in the company
  Your manual tells you which scope applies to each feature you can use.
  If a screen or record is not visible to you, it is almost always because
  it is outside your scope — that is expected, not a bug.

HOW THE SYSTEM IS LAID OUT (true for every role)
  - Top bar: global search (click the search box or press Ctrl/Cmd+K),
    a bell icon for notifications, a sun/moon icon to switch light/dark
    mode, and your name (profile, password, logout) on the far right.
  - Left sidebar: your modules, grouped by area (Sales, Money, Delivery,
    People, Insights, Admin). Only the modules you have access to appear.
  - Every list screen (leads, invoices, tasks, etc.) has filters at the
    top, a "Saved filters" option to re-use a filter combination, and an
    Export button (large exports may need a manager's approval — see
    "Approvals" in your manual).
  - Clicking anywhere on a row/card (not just its name) opens the record.
  - Destructive actions (archive, cancel, delete, write-off, reject) ask
    for a reason before they go through. Always give a real reason — it
    is kept permanently in that record's history and in the audit log.
  - A record's "Timeline" / "Activity" tab is the full history of what
    happened to it, who did it, and when. This is how you "track" things
    in this system — read it before asking a colleague what happened.

GETTING HELP
  - If a button is missing or you get "You do not have access to...",
    that is a permissions issue — see the Troubleshooting section at the
    end of your manual, then contact your Owner / Super Admin.
  - If something looks broken (wrong numbers, a stuck record, an error
    toast), screenshot it and send it to your administrator with the
    record's ID/number — do not try to fix data directly in two places.
