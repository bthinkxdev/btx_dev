================================================================================
BTHINKX BUSINESS OS — USER MANUAL
ROLE: HR Manager
================================================================================
Your access: COMPANY scope and full control of Employees (incl. reveal
sensitive fields, archive, export), Attendance (incl. approve/import),
Leave (incl. approve/configure), Payroll (create/edit); COMPANY view on
Commissions and Reports; OWN Expenses.
Your home screen after login: Dashboards -> HR.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIGNING IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enter your email/username + password, complete 2FA if prompted. Forgot your
password? Ask your Owner — you cannot self-reset.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HR DASHBOARD — Dashboards -> HR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Headcount, upcoming joiners (next 30 days), exits/notice period in progress,
attendance exceptions. Use this every morning to catch anything that needs
action today (e.g. someone's last working day is today, or an unexplained
absence streak).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EMPLOYEES — People -> Employees
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Status lifecycle
  Onboarding -> Active -> On notice -> Exited (or On leave (long) alongside)

3.2 Onboarding a new employee
  1) Employees -> Add employee: personal details, employment type (Full
     time / Part time / Contract / Intern), designation, department, team,
     manager, joining date, shift.
  2) Sensitive fields (salary, bank details, PAN/tax IDs) are entered here
     too, but only visible to roles with "reveal sensitive" access — that's
     you and Owner. Double-check these carefully; they drive payroll.
  3) Assign a Shift and, if relevant, link their system user account so
     they can log in and use self-service screens.
  4) Checklist items (if your onboarding checklist is configured) track
     document collection, asset handover, etc. — tick off as completed.

3.3 Transfers, offboarding, exits
  On an employee's detail page, "⋮" menu:
  - "Transfer" — move them to a new team/department/manager (effective
    date), keeps full history under one employee record.
  - "Offboard" — start the exit process: set last working day, reason,
    moves status to On notice.
  - "Exit now" — immediate exit (status -> Exited) for cases with no
    notice period.
  - "Reveal" — temporarily reveal sensitive fields (salary/bank/tax ID) on
    screen; every reveal is logged in the audit trail, so only do this when
    you actually need to check/update the value.
  - "Activate" — reactivate a record (e.g. correcting an accidental exit).
Must do: process an exit the same day it's confirmed — a stale Active
  employee still shows on rosters, attendance expectations and payroll
  drafts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ATTENDANCE — People -> Attendance
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Statuses per day: Present / Absent / Half day / On leave (and others your
company has configured). You can:
  - "Mark" attendance manually for someone (e.g. a field visit not caught
    by check-in/out).
  - Review and approve/reject "Correction" requests employees raise when
    their own check-in/out was missed or wrong.
  - "Import" a bulk attendance file (e.g. from a biometric device export).
Must do: review and clear correction requests within the same pay cycle —
  unresolved corrections distort payroll calculations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. LEAVE — People -> Leave
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Status lifecycle (leave requests)
  Pending -> Approved / Rejected (or Cancelled by the employee before
  decision)

5.2 Approving leave — People -> HR approvals (or the Leave list filtered to
  Pending)
  Open a request, check it against the employee's leave balance (shown on
  the request) and team coverage, then Approve or Reject (reason
  recommended). "Adjust" lets you manually correct someone's leave balance
  (e.g. a carry-forward or a manual grant) — always with a note explaining
  why.

5.3 Configuring leave policy — People -> Leave -> Configure (leave types,
  entitlement, carry-forward rules). Agree any policy change with Owner
  before applying it company-wide.

5.4 Holidays — People -> Holidays
  Maintain the company holiday calendar; this feeds both the Leave and
  Attendance screens so holidays aren't miscounted as absences.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PAYROLL — People -> Payroll
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Status lifecycle
  Draft -> In review -> Pending approval -> Approved and locked -> Paid
  (or Partially paid / Reversed)

6.2 Running payroll for a period
  1) Payroll -> Add run (or it's auto-created from a schedule) for the
     period (first day of the payroll month).
  2) "Recalculate" pulls in attendance, leave, salary structure and any
     adjustments to compute each employee's pay.
  3) "Review" the run — check individual payslips for anything unusual
     (big swings from last period, missing employees, wrong structure).
  4) Add one-off "Adjustments" (Payroll -> Adjustment) for bonuses,
     deductions, arrears not covered by the standard structure, before
     recalculating again.
  5) "Submit" moves it to Pending approval — a Finance/Accounting or Owner
     sign-off is required before payment; you cannot both prepare and pay a
     run yourself. This is a deliberate control, not a bug.
  6) Once approved, generate the "Payment file" for your bank, then
     "Mark paid" once the bank transfer is actually done, or "Release" to
     make payslips visible to employees.
  7) "Reverse" only for a genuine error after approval, with a reason — it
     re-opens the run for correction.
Must do: never mark a run "Paid" before the bank transfer has actually
  gone through — employees see payslips as soon as it's released.

6.3 Salary structures — Payroll -> Structures
  Components (basic, HRA, allowances, deductions) per structure; assign a
  structure to each employee on their record. Changes apply from the
  effective date you set, not retroactively to already-paid periods.

6.4 Payslips — Payroll -> My payslips (your own) / from a run's detail for
  others. PDF download available per payslip.

6.5 Commissions — People -> Payroll -> Commissions (view)
  See commission entries flowing from Sales approvals (Accrued -> Approved
  -> Paid / Clawed back) so you can include approved ones in the right
  payroll run.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. REPORTS — Reports (company scope)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Headcount, attrition, attendance exceptions, leave utilisation, payroll
cost. Export or schedule any of these for your own monthly HR review.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. YOUR OWN EXPENSES (own scope)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
People -> Expenses -> Add expense: same self-service flow every employee
uses (choose kind, category, amount, attach receipt, submit) — see manual
09, section 7, for the exact steps. Someone else approves your own claims.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. APPROVALS — YOUR INBOX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Leave requests and attendance corrections needing a decision land in
Approvals -> Mine (or the dedicated HR approvals screen). Clear daily —
unresolved leave requests leave employees uncertain and distort attendance/
payroll data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. YOUR DAILY / WEEKLY / MONTHLY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Daily:
  [ ] HR approvals / Approvals -> Mine — clear leave & correction requests.
  [ ] Dashboards -> HR — any exit due today, any attendance exception?
Weekly:
  [ ] Employees -> anyone due to move from On notice to Exited?
  [ ] Attendance corrections backlog.
Monthly:
  [ ] Run payroll: recalculate -> review -> adjustments -> submit.
  [ ] Confirm the prior run reached Paid status.
  [ ] Reports -> Headcount / Attrition / Leave utilisation reviewed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I can't see an employee's salary/bank details" -> use "Reveal" on their
  record; if still hidden, your role may have lost field access — check
  with Owner.
"Payroll run won't let me mark it paid" -> it must reach Approved and
  locked first via Finance/Accounting or Owner sign-off — you cannot
  self-approve your own prepared run.
"An employee's leave balance looks wrong" -> check Leave -> their history
  for any manual Adjustment, and confirm the right Leave type/policy is
  assigned to them.
"Attendance import didn't match some rows" -> check employee IDs in your
  source file match exactly; fix and re-import just the failed rows.
