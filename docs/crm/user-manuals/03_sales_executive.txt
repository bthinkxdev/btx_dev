================================================================================
BTHINKX BUSINESS OS — USER MANUAL
ROLE: Sales Executive
================================================================================
Your access: OWN scope only — the leads, deals, quotations, payment links,
payments, clients, commissions and reports that belong to you. You cannot
see or act on another rep's records.
Your home screen after login: My Day.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIGNING IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enter your email/username + password, complete 2FA if prompted. Forgot your
password? You cannot self-reset — ask your Sales Manager or Owner.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MY DAY — your starting screen every morning
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
My Day lists, for you only:
  - Overdue follow-ups (red) — act on these first.
  - Today's follow-ups.
  - Anything else due today across your leads.
Click any item to jump straight into that lead and log the call/meeting/
note, or complete/postpone the follow-up from there.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LEADS — Sales -> Leads -> All leads
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 What you see
  Only leads where you are the owner. New leads are assigned to you by your
  Sales Manager or Telecaller, or you create your own.

3.2 Creating a lead
  1) "Add lead" -> fill name, phone, company, service interested, source.
  2) The system flags likely duplicates as you type — check them before
     saving to avoid double-working the same enquiry.
  3) Save — you are automatically the owner.

3.3 Working a lead (the stage pipeline)
  New -> Contacted -> Nurturing -> Qualified -> Quotation prepared ->
  Quotation sent -> Negotiation -> Won / Lost
  On the lead's detail page:
  - Quick log: Log call, Log meeting, Add note, Log email or WhatsApp —
    use these every time you actually speak to or message the prospect.
  - "Change stage" moves it forward (or back) in the pipeline.
  - "Schedule follow-up" sets your next action and due date/time — this is
    required; an open lead cannot sit with no scheduled next action.
  - "Mark won" when the deal closes; "Mark lost" requires picking a real
    lost reason (Price too high / Competitor / No budget / No response /
    Not ready / Scope mismatch / Invalid number / Duplicate / Other).
  Must do: log every contact. Your manager and the Owner dashboard read
  straight off this activity log, not off memory or chat messages.

3.4 Priority
  Set each lead Hot / Warm / Cold honestly — it decides what shows first
  under the "High priority" quick filter and who gets chased by your
  manager if it slips.

3.5 Tracking your own pipeline
  Quick filters on the list: My leads, Due today, Overdue, No activity 3
  days, New in last 24h, High priority, Open, Not yet contacted,
  First-response SLA breached. Use "Follow-up calendar" (Leads -> Follow-up
  calendar) to see your schedule on a calendar instead of a list.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. YOUR PIPELINE AND DEALS — Sales -> Pipeline
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Kanban view of your own open deals by stage with weighted value. Drag a
card to change its stage (same as "Change stage" on the lead). Your numbers
against target appear on your own dashboard tile where your manager has
set one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CLIENTS — Sales -> Clients
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clients you own (usually created when your lead is marked Won). Client
detail shows every quotation/invoice/payment/project tied to them in one
place — use it before any call so you walk in knowing the full history.
Create a client directly from Clients -> Add client if one didn't come from
a lead (e.g. a walk-in).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. QUOTATIONS — Sales -> Quotations
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Status lifecycle
  Draft -> Awaiting approval -> Approved -> Sent -> Viewed -> Accepted
  (or Rejected / Expired / Superseded / Converted / Void)

6.2 Step by step
  1) From the lead, click "Create quotation" (only enabled once the lead is
     Qualified) or Quotations -> Add quotation.
  2) Add line items from the service catalogue; set quantity and any
     discount. Totals (incl. GST) calculate live.
  3) Save as Draft and review. Submitting:
     - within your discount limit -> goes straight to Approved.
     - above your limit -> goes to "Awaiting approval" in your Sales
       Manager's Approvals inbox; you'll be notified once decided.
  4) "Send" — delivers the quotation to the client and creates a trackable
     link; status becomes Sent, then Viewed automatically once they open
     it.
  5) Record the outcome: "Accept" or "Reject" (pick the real rejection
     reason) if the client told you directly rather than acting on the
     public link themselves.
  You can also: "Duplicate" (reuse as a starting point for a new one),
  "New version" (revise a sent quotation — this supersedes the old one),
  "Compare" versions, "Resend", "Cancel" your own pending-approval request,
  or "Void" it (with a reason) if it's dead.
Must do: chase anything sitting in "Sent" near its validity date — do not
let quotations silently expire; either close the deal, extend/re-send, or
mark it lost on the lead.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PAYMENT LINKS — Money -> Collections -> Payment links
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Create a payment link from an approved quotation/invoice to collect money
without waiting for a full invoice: choose purpose (Full balance / Advance
percent / Milestone / Custom partial amount) and payment method. Above your
limit it needs your manager's approval before it goes Active. "Cancel" it
if it's no longer needed (reason required).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. PAYMENTS AND REFUNDS (your own clients only)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Payments -> Payments: record a payment you personally received (e.g. a
cheque or bank transfer the client told you about) — it goes in as
"Pending verification" until Finance/Accounting clears it; you cannot
verify your own entries. Refunds: you can raise a refund request for your
own client's payment; Finance/Accounting approves and processes it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. INVOICES (view only)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Money -> Invoices shows invoices for your own clients so you can answer "has
this been billed / paid" questions. You cannot create or edit invoices —
that is Finance's job once a deal is won (ask your manager or Finance to
raise one, referencing the won quotation).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. COMMISSIONS — People -> Payroll -> Commissions (view only)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
See your own commission entries and their status: Accrued -> Approved ->
Paid (or Clawed back). Approval is done by your Sales Manager once the
underlying deal and payment are confirmed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. REPORTS (your own numbers)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reports shows your own activity/performance reports — conversion rate,
average response time, win/loss, quota attainment. Use these in your own
1:1s with your manager instead of guessing your numbers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. YOUR OWN LEAVE, ATTENDANCE AND EXPENSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
People -> Leave: apply for leave (pick type and dates, submit — goes to
your manager to approve/reject).
People -> Attendance: check in/out; request a correction if a check-in was
missed or wrong (goes for approval).
People -> Expenses: Expenses -> Add expense — choose kind (Expense / Vendor
bill / Reimbursement), category, amount, attach the receipt, and Submit.
Status moves Draft -> Submitted -> Approved -> Paid (or Rejected /
Correction requested — fix and resubmit if so).
Must do: attach a receipt to every expense claim before submitting; claims
without proof are routinely sent back for correction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. YOUR DAILY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] My Day — clear overdue follow-ups first, then today's.
  [ ] Every lead you touched today has Quick-log activity recorded.
  [ ] Every open lead has a scheduled next action.
  [ ] Quotations sitting in "Sent" — any need chasing today?
  [ ] Notifications bell — any approval decision or SLA warning for you?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I can't find a lead I was told about" -> it may not be assigned to you yet
  — ask your Sales Manager or Telecaller to assign it; you only see leads
  where you are the owner.
"Create quotation is disabled on my lead" -> the lead must reach
  "Qualified" stage first.
"My quotation/payment link is stuck" -> check its status; "Awaiting
  approval" means it's sitting with your manager, not an error.
"I can't verify a payment I recorded" -> correct — only Finance/Accounting
  verifies payments, by design, as a control.
"I can't see/edit an invoice" -> you have view-only on invoices; ask
  Finance to make the change.
