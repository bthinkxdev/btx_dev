================================================================================
BTHINKX BUSINESS OS — USER MANUAL
ROLE: Owner / Super Admin
================================================================================
Your access: EVERYTHING, at COMPANY scope. You configure the system, manage
users and roles, approve what others cannot, and are the final escalation
point for every module below.
Your home screen after login: Owner Dashboard (Dashboards -> Owner).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIGNING IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) Go to the login page and enter your email/username and password.
2) If your account has too many wrong attempts, it locks temporarily — wait
   for the time shown on screen, do not keep retrying.
3) If two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled on your account, enter the
   6-digit code from your authenticator app within 5 minutes of the prompt.
4) You land on the Owner Dashboard. Use the profile menu (top right) to view
   your profile, change password, or log out.
Must do: keep 2FA enabled on your own account — you hold the keys to every
other account in the company.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. YOUR DASHBOARDS (Dashboards menu)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can open any dashboard in the system, and switch between them with the
"Switch" button on each dashboard page:
  - Owner       : cash collected, outstanding receivables, revenue, gross/
                  operating profit, cash & bank balance, pipeline, MRR,
                  renewals due, approvals waiting > 24h — the whole business
                  on one page.
  - Sales       : pipeline, win rate, lead response times, targets vs actual.
  - Finance     : receivables ageing, collections, GST/TDS position.
  - Delivery    : milestones due, open blockers, team utilisation.
  - HR          : headcount, upcoming joiners/exits, attendance exceptions.
  Each dashboard has a date-range picker (Today / This week / This month /
  Last month / This quarter / This financial year, or a custom From-To) and
  a "Configure" button to choose which stat cards show for your company.
How to track the business day to day: open the Owner Dashboard every
morning, check "Approvals waiting over 24h" and anything red/overdue, then
drill into the module behind any number that looks wrong (click the card).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. USER, ROLE AND TEAM ADMINISTRATION (Settings)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the area only you (and anyone you grant 'settings' access) can see.

3.1 Users — Settings -> Users
  - List shows every user, their role(s), team and status.
  - Open a user to: change their roles, reset 2FA, lock/unlock the account,
    deactivate (never hard-delete a user who has created records — archive
    instead, since their history must stay attributable).
  Must do: when someone leaves the company, deactivate their account the
  same day and reassign their open leads/deals/tasks to someone active.

3.2 Roles — Settings -> Roles
  - Each role is a named bundle of module -> action -> scope grants (the
    same model this manual is built on: view/create/edit/archive/approve/
    export/assign/cancel/refund/override/reveal_sensitive/import/configure,
    each at own/team/department/company).
  - Use "New role" to create a custom role, or open an existing one to
    adjust its grants and which sensitive field groups it can see (Salary,
    Bank details, PAN/GSTIN, Gateway credentials, Internal notes, Cost
    price & margin).
  Must do: change permissions here, never by asking a developer to hard-code
  an exception — every grant you create is visible and auditable.

3.3 Teams and Departments — Settings -> Teams
  - Define teams and department structure; this drives what "team" and
    "department" scope actually mean for every Sales Manager, Project
    Manager, etc. Keep manager assignments current — scope rules depend on
    them directly.

3.4 Company, Tax and Gateway settings — Settings -> Company / Tax / Gateway
  - Company: legal name, address, logo, financial year, default currency.
  - Tax: GST registration, tax categories, TDS sections, and the document
    numbering series for leads/quotations/invoices/credit notes/payment
    receipts (Settings -> Tax -> series). Numbering series must never be
    edited once documents have been issued against them — create a new
    series instead.
  - Gateway: payment gateway credentials (card/UPI/netbanking provider
    keys). Only an Owner should hold 'gateway_credentials' field access.

3.5 Message templates — Settings -> Templates
  - Edit the wording of system emails/WhatsApp messages (quotation sent,
    invoice reminder, payment confirmation, etc.). Use the merge fields
    shown on the editor; test with "Send test" before relying on a change.

3.6 Automation / Jobs — Settings -> Jobs
  - Shows background jobs (recurring invoices, reminders, bank imports,
    report schedules). A failed job is shown in red with a retry action.
  Must do: check this weekly; a silently-failing job (e.g. invoice reminders)
  is invisible everywhere else in the app.

3.7 Audit log — Settings -> Audit
  - Full, immutable history of who did what, when, from where. Filter by
    user, module or date. This is your record of truth in any dispute.

3.8 Security events — Settings -> Security
  - Failed logins, lockouts, 2FA failures, forbidden-access attempts (someone
    hitting a URL they have no permission for returns a 403 and logs here).
  - "Unlock" button releases an account that locked itself out.
  Must do: review this at least weekly; repeated forbidden-access attempts
  from one account is worth a direct conversation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SALES: LEADS, PIPELINE, QUOTATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
See the Sales Manager and Sales Executive manuals (02, 03) for full
step-by-step detail — as Owner you have the same screens at COMPANY scope,
plus the ability to re-assign anything, override discount/approval limits,
and reconfigure lead sources, campaigns and sales targets (Sales ->
Targets).

Key things only you should decide:
  - Discount/approval thresholds (set in Settings -> Policy): the amount
    above which a quotation or payment link needs manager approval.
  - Lead sources/channels and campaigns (Leads -> Sources and campaigns):
    keep this list clean — reporting by source is only useful if reps are
    not inventing new source names ad hoc.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MONEY: QUOTATIONS, PAYMENT LINKS, PAYMENTS, INVOICES, REFUNDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full detail is in the Finance Executive and Accountant manuals (05, 06).
As Owner, you additionally:
  - Approve/override quotations and invoices beyond normal limits (the
    "Override" action on a quotation/invoice sets its totals or status
    outside the normal workflow — use sparingly, it is logged).
  - See every payment verification queue and every refund request company-
    wide, not just your own team's.
  - Can issue, cancel, write off or apply advances to any invoice.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. ACCOUNTING AND BANKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full detail in the Accountant manual (06). As Owner you can additionally
configure the chart of accounts, open/close accounting periods, and
override posted journal entries if a correction is unavoidable — always via
a reversing entry, never by editing a posted line.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DELIVERY: CONTRACTS, PROJECTS, TASKS, TICKETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full detail in the Project Manager manual (08). You can create and approve
any contract/project/ticket company-wide, and see delivery utilisation
across every team from the Delivery dashboard.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. EXPENSES AND VENDORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full step-by-step in the Finance Executive manual (05). You have company-
wide view/create/edit/approve/export on expenses and vendors; use this to
spot-check the finance team's approvals periodically.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. HR, ATTENDANCE, LEAVE, PAYROLL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full step-by-step in the HR Manager manual (07). As Owner you have the same
access, plus final sign-off is still required from Finance/Accounting
before a payroll run is paid — the system does not let a single role both
prepare and pay a payroll run without that second approval.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. REPORTS AND THE REPORT CATALOGUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reports -> Report catalogue lists every pre-built report (sales funnel,
source performance, SLA breaches, receivables ageing, GST/TDS, utilisation,
headcount, etc.), grouped by area. Open any report, set a date range or
preset (Today / This week / This month / Last month / This quarter / This
financial year), and:
  - Export CSV directly from the report toolbar.
  - Click "Schedule" to have it emailed to a chosen group on a recurring
    basis (daily/weekly/monthly) — manage existing schedules from the same
    screen.
  - Click any row to drill into the underlying record where the report
    supports it (most do).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. APPROVALS — YOUR INBOX (top bar / Approvals)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Any approval that escalates above a manager's own limit lands with you.
  - Tab "Mine": everything waiting on your decision. Filter "Overdue" to
    see only items waiting more than 24 hours.
  - Tab "Requested": approvals you yourself raised.
  - Tab "History": decisions you have made.
  Open an item, read its detail panel and history, then Approve / Reject
  (with a comment) or, if you raised it yourself, Cancel.
Must do: clear your "Mine" tab daily. A stuck approval blocks the record
(a quotation cannot be sent, a payment link cannot be collected, an invoice
cannot be issued) until you act.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The bell icon shows unread items (approvals, overdue follow-ups, SLA
breaches, failed jobs). Click one to jump straight to the record, "Snooze"
to come back to it later, or "Mark all read" once you have triaged them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. YOUR DAILY / WEEKLY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Daily:
  [ ] Owner Dashboard — scan every stat card for anything red/unexpected.
  [ ] Approvals -> Mine — clear to zero (or a conscious, explained backlog).
  [ ] Notifications bell — triage.
Weekly:
  [ ] Settings -> Jobs — any failed background job? Retry or escalate.
  [ ] Settings -> Security — review failed logins / forbidden attempts.
  [ ] Reports -> Receivables ageing — anything sliding past terms?
  [ ] Reports -> Renewals due (60 days) — contracts at risk of lapsing.
Monthly:
  [ ] Accounting -> Periods — soft-close last month once reconciled.
  [ ] HR -> Payroll — confirm the run was prepared, approved and paid.
  [ ] Settings -> Audit — spot-check a sample of sensitive-field reveals.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"User says they can't see/do something" -> Settings -> Users -> open them
  -> check their role(s) and scope. Fix at the role level, not by creating
  one-off exceptions per user where avoidable.
"A number on a dashboard looks wrong" -> click into the underlying report
  or list and filter to the same date range; the dashboard and report use
  the same data, so a mismatch is usually a date-range or scope difference.
"A record is stuck mid-workflow" -> open its Timeline/history tab first;
  it will show exactly which approval or status step it is waiting on.
"Someone got a 403 (forbidden)" -> expected behaviour if they lack the
  permission; check Settings -> Security to confirm it logged, then decide
  whether to grant the permission via their role.
